## What Is a Prompt?

A **prompt** is the instruction or question you give to an AI model.

**Bad Prompt:**
```
Tell me about trees.
```

**Good Prompt:**
```
Explain the water cycle using the growth of an oak tree
as an example. Use 3-4 sentences suitable for 9th graders.
```

The more specific and clear, the better the response!

---

## Prompt Engineering Patterns

| Pattern | Example | When to Use |
|---------|---------|------------|
| **Zero-Shot** | "Write a poem about AI" | Quick, simple tasks |
| **Few-Shot** | Show 2 examples, then ask for more | Complex or unusual tasks |
| **Chain-of-Thought** | "Step 1: ... Step 2: ..." | Problems needing reasoning |
| **Role-Based** | "You are a professional editor..." | Need specific style/perspective |

---

## Key Elements of a Good Prompt

1. **Context** — What should the AI know?
2. **Task** — What exactly do you want it to do?
3. **Format** — How should the answer look?
4. **Constraints** — Any limits? (length, tone, audience)

**Example:**
> Context: You are a teacher.
> Task: Write a quiz question.
> Format: Multiple choice with 4 options.
> Constraint: For 10th grade biology, no more than 50 words.

## Does the Output Actually Work?

Ask yourself:

- **Validity:** Is it technically correct and coherent?
- **Relevance:** Does it answer what I asked?
- **Authenticity:** Is it actually from the AI, not copied?
- **Bias:** Does it show unfair treatment of any group?
- **Hallucinations:** Did it make up facts that sound real?

---

## Common AI Problems

| Problem | What to Look For | Example |
|---------|------------------|---------|
| **Hallucination** | Made-up facts, fake sources | "The 1987 World Series was won by the Blue Jays" (wrong!) |
| **Bias** | Unfair stereotypes | Only showing one gender in certain jobs |
| **Vagueness** | Too generic, not useful | "Technology is important" |
| **Outdated** | Old information | "The current president is..." |

---

## How to Improve Your Prompts

1. **Test and iterate** — Try different versions
2. **Be more specific** — Add more context and details
3. **Ask for reasoning** — "Explain your thinking step-by-step"
4. **Set expectations** — Tell it the format/length you want
5. **Check the output** — Always review for accuracy and bias

---

## Summary

**Good prompts = Better AI responses**

- Use context, task, format, and constraints
- Choose the right pattern (zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-based)
- Always evaluate output for validity, relevance, authenticity, bias, and hallucinations
- Iterate and improve

Your prompt engineering skills directly impact AI quality!
